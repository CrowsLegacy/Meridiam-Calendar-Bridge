# Privacy Policy — Meridiam Calendar Bridge

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Meridiam Calendar Bridge is a companion application developed by Crow's Legacy to connect the Meridiam widget with supported calendar services, including Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook.

## Calendar Data

Meridiam Calendar Bridge accesses calendar information only as necessary to provide calendar synchronization and display functionality within Meridiam.

Depending on the calendar provider and the permissions granted by the user, this may include information such as event titles, dates, times, and other calendar-related information required for the application to function.

## Third-Party Services

When connecting a calendar account, authentication and authorization may be handled by the respective third-party provider.

Supported services may include:

- Google Calendar
- Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365

Use of these services is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of their respective providers.

## Data Collection

Crow's Legacy does not sell calendar data or personal information obtained through Meridiam Calendar Bridge.

Meridiam Calendar Bridge is designed to use calendar information for the purpose of providing its synchronization and display features.

## Account Access

Users control which calendar accounts they connect to Meridiam Calendar Bridge and may revoke access through their respective Google or Microsoft account settings.

## Data Sharing

Crow's Legacy does not sell or rent users' calendar information to third parties.

Information may be exchanged with Google, Microsoft, or other supported services when necessary to authenticate the user or provide the requested calendar integration.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit access to calendar information to what is necessary for Meridiam Calendar Bridge to operate.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Meridiam Calendar Bridge evolves or additional calendar providers and features are introduced.

Changes will be published in this repository.
